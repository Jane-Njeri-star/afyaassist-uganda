How can we help?

How do I track my medicine order?

Go to My Orders or enter your order number on the Track page. You will see real-time status including dispatch and delivery updates.

My delivery is late — what should I do?

Use Get Help → Delivery → My delivery is late. Provide your order number so we can check with the courier immediately.

How long do refunds take?

Refunds are normally processed within 3–7 business days depending on your payment method (Mobile Money or card).

I was charged twice

Open a Payment ticket and select “I was charged twice”. Attach any screenshots of the double charge for faster resolution.

📍 Geographic Problem

🟢 Positive Trend

Recommended Actions

01 — Investigate Delivery

Delivery complaints are concentrated in Kampala and Wakiso.

Recommended action: Review delivery partners, delivery routes and customer communication in these locations.

02 — Improve Refund Communication

Refund-related tickets have the lowest satisfaction.

Recommended action: Improve refund-status communication and provide customers with clearer expected timelines.

03 — Improve Self-Service

Payment questions represent a significant portion of support requests.

Recommended action: Create a clearer payment troubleshooting guide and FAQ.
